# Planning Beyond Predictability
"LayScen"-Layered Adaptive Scenario Planning, An Integrated Framework for Urban Resilience

**📄 [Download the White Paper (PDF)](https://ranuagg.github.io/Urban-Planning-and-Resilience/Planning%20Beyond%20Predictability_LayScen.pdf)**
On mobile, open the downloaded PDF from your Downloads/Files folder.

The challenge isn't just uncertainty alone — it's also the fragmentation in how we respond to it. 
I am proposing "LayScen" (Layered Adaptive Scenario Planning) to address both. LayScen is a practitioner-oriented framework and methodology documented in this white paper

What's inside:
→ An eight-phase workflow — from scanning the drivers of change to institutionalizing adaptive practice
→ Scenario planning, adaptive planning, and layered systems thinking integrated into one coordinated workflow
→ A real-world illustrative walkthrough, using San Francisco's Climate Action Plan and Hazards and Climate Resilience Plan
→ Where it's headed next — a portable application currently in development

I believe, it will be specially useful for city and regional planners, resilience and sustainability planners, hazard mitigation and infrastructure planners, agencies, consultants, and the community partners they serve.
